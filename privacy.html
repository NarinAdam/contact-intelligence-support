Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Overview

Contact Intelligence is designed to help users organize and analyze contacts while keeping contact information on their device.

Contacts permission

The app requests access to Contacts to display contact information, identify phone number countries, organize contacts with tags, and provide related app features.

Data collection

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share users' contact information. Contact information, tags, notes, favorites, and reminders are processed and stored locally on the user's device.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store payment card information.

Third-party services

When a user chooses to call, send a message, email, or open WhatsApp, the selected service is handled by the relevant app or system service under its own privacy practices.
